Safety & privacy

Built to be trusted by the whole neighbourhood.

A local app only works when people feel safe using it. So privacy and moderation were designed in from day one — not added later.

Report anything

Every post has a report button. Three reports from established accounts hide it automatically.

Verified badges

Service providers, tutors, rentals and businesses verify with a CNIC photo and selfie, checked by our moderators.

Real moderators

A moderation team reviews reports, suspends abusers and keeps a permanent audit trail.

No ads. No data selling.

Private by default

Your location, your call

Locked-down database

Leave anytime
